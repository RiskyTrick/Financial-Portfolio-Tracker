How Your Data is Saved

Portfolio Tracker stores your data in Firebase Cloud — synced securely to your account, accessible from any device.

Key facts:
☁️ Synced to cloud on every save
✅ Access from any device with your account
🔒 Data is private to your Firebase account
💾 Also cached locally for offline fallback
⚙️ Storage key (local cache): fintrack_v2

To protect your data: Use ⬇ Export Backup for extra safety. This downloads a .txt JSON file. Re-import anytime via ⬆ Import Data.

Your data is synced every time you save. Sign in on any device to access it.
